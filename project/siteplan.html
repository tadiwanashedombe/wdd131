TheGalleRY Plan

1.Site Name

The site is going to be name TheGalleRY. I think that this name is very on point as to what the website will be focused on. The name makes it obvious that we will be dealing with photos.

2. Purpose

TheGalleRY will be focused on helping users to see and manage their photos.It will allows users to add and remove photos from their collection. These collection will be used to group related photos and user can create and manage their collections.

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1:

"I just came back from a vacation trip with hundreds of photos on my phone. How do I organize them into collections of just the picture i just took at the beach."

Scenario 2:

"I created a collection last month for my client but since i sent them their photos I can delete them from my last month collection."

Scenario 3:

"I have a lot of photos in different collection how can i see them all at once in one place."
